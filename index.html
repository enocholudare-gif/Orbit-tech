Install Orbit Tech App

Install this Progressive Web App directly to your device home screen.

On iPhone / iPad (Safari):

  1. Tap the Share button in Safari's bottom toolbar.
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen .
  3. Tap Add at top right to complete install.

On Android / Chrome:

  1. Click Install App on the prompt, or open Chrome's Three Dots ⋮ menu.
  2. Tap Install App or Add to Home Screen.
  3. Launch Orbit Tech from your app launcher!
